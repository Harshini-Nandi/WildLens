# WildBuddy — 3-Minute Hackathon Demonstration Script

This script is designed for presenting WildBuddy to judges and audiences at open-source AI hackathons.

---

## ⏱️ [0:00 – 0:40] The Hook & The Problem

> *"Judges, let's look around this room. How many hours have you spent staring at screens today? Most modern AI applications are designed to glue our eyes to chat windows and feeds for longer.
>
> We asked a different question: **Can an open-source AI model actually help us spend LESS time on screens and MORE time connecting with the living world?**
>
> Meet **WildBuddy — Your Offline AI Nature Companion**. Our core philosophy is simple: **Less scrolling. More exploring.**"*

---

## ⏱️ [0:40 – 1:30] Live Specimen Identification with Local Open AI

*(Open the AI Identifier screen on laptop or mobile view)*

> *"When people walk in nature, they often spot an intriguing leaf or wild flower and wonder what it is. With WildBuddy, we don't send their photos to a privacy-invasive corporate cloud or pay expensive API fees.
>
> Instead, WildBuddy connects directly to **Ollama** running an open-weight vision model right on this machine — such as Google's Gemma 3 or LLaVA.
>
> Watch this: Let's photograph this leaf.
>
> In seconds, the local vision model suggests the common and scientific name, extracts key visible characteristics like margin serrations and veining, provides an ecological story, and gives a practical outdoor safety caution.
>
> With one tap, we save this observation directly to our private, offline Nature Journal."*

---

## ⏱️ [1:30 – 2:15] Screen-Minimal Outdoor Missions & Offline PWA

*(Switch to Outdoor Missions screen)*

> *"Now here is what truly makes WildBuddy unique: our **Outdoor Adventure Generator**.
>
> Rather than keeping you on your device, you select your available time — say 15 minutes in a neighborhood park — and your interest.
>
> The model generates an observational mission: find two contrasting leaf shapes, listen silently for 60 seconds, and document an ant trail.
>
> When you press **Start Adventure**, WildBuddy switches into **Screen-Off Mode**. It literally instructs you: *'Put your phone in your pocket, walk outside, and listen.'*
>
> You complete the tasks in the real world, return, check off your discoveries, and celebrate your achievement with confetti."*

---

## ⏱️ [2:15 – 3:00] Data Sovereignty & Offline Resilience

*(Switch to DevTools Network Offline mode or show Journal & Progress)*

> *"Notice our metrics: Streaks and outdoor minutes are calculated purely from real local IndexedDB records — no fake numbers.
>
> And look at this: even if we are deep in a remote national park with **zero cellular coverage**, the Service Worker and IndexedDB keep the entire app functional. The client-side deterministic generator generates missions, and our journal is 100% accessible.
>
> WildBuddy proves that open-source AI is not just about building bigger chatbots — it's about empowering humans to rediscover the real, physical world around them.
>
> Thank you, and remember to touch grass today!"*
